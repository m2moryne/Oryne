# Code of conduct

Oryne is built in the open, and we want everyone who takes part to feel welcome: in issues, pull
requests, discussions and anywhere else the project meets.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Stellar, crypto or open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Accept that maintainers sometimes say no, and explain why when they do.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or language and images of that kind.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Scams, or promoting tokens or investments.

## If something happens

Tell the maintainers through the [contact page](https://oryne.org/contact). Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments or code, or block people who don't follow this code.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
